Rayaan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Rayaan is published by Blindspill (LAMENTO TRADING L.L.C), Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and built on one principle: what happens on your phone stays on your phone.

1. What we collect

  • Nothing on our servers. Rayaan has no accounts, no logins, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • All your personal data — bookmarks, salah / fasting / sadaqah logs, hifz progress, prayer settings, reading goal, reciter preference — is stored in a local database on your device only. We never receive it.

2. Location

  • If you grant location access, your latitude/longitude is used on-device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not transmitted to us.
  • For the optional Nearby Mosques feature, your coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API and the Foursquare Places API to find mosques around you. No identifier is attached.
  • You can also set your city manually and use the app with no location permission at all.

3. Network requests

Rayaan contacts a small number of public, read-only services, and only when you trigger them (open a surah, play audio, calculate Zakat):

  • quran.com API and alquran.cloud — Quran text, translations, tajweed, search, page layout.
  • Quran audio CDNs (verses.quran.com, mirrors.quranicaudio.com) — reciter audio streaming and downloads.
  • hisnmuslim.com — Hisnul Muslim supplications.
  • gold-api.com — live gold/silver prices for the Zakat calculator.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass and Foursquare Places — nearby mosques (see section 2).
  • Font/data CDNs (raw.githubusercontent.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net) — Quran fonts and reference data.

These services see only standard request metadata (your IP address and the URL requested), the same as any web browser. Rayaan attaches no account identifier, advertising ID, or tracking cookie.

4. Notifications

Adhan and reminder notifications are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. Rayaan has no server able to push messages to your device.

5. Backups

  • Automatic — your local database is part of your device's standard system backup (iCloud for iOS; Google Auto Backup for Android), managed by your operating system, not by us. Downloaded audio and font caches are excluded from backup.
  • Manual — you can export a copy of your data from within the app and choose where to save it via the system share sheet.

6. Third-party SDKs

Rayaan contains no advertising and no analytics. Its components are limited to the Flutter platform and open-source libraries for audio playback, local storage, and local notifications, plus the public data services listed above.

7. Children

Rayaan is suitable for all ages. No data is collected from anyone, including children.
